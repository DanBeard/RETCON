MicroRETCON — Flash a Transport Node (RAK4631)

What this is

This flashes the MicroRETCON transport firmware onto a RAK4631 module (RAK19003 base) — the solar-powered 24/7 LoRa+BLE+USB transport node. Plug the board into this Pi's USB port; the Pi does the rest.

Step 1 — put the board in bootloader mode

Double-tap the reset button on the RAK19003. The board re-enumerates as a USB mass-storage drive (its built-in UF2 bootloader).
